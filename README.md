## 🕹️ About

Toxic Crusaders is a side-scrolling beat ’em up set in Tromaville, where the Crusaders battle Dr. Killemoff, Czar Zosta, Radiation Rangers, thugs, and zombie chickens. This unofficial Windows build surfaced early, preserving the rough edges of a colorful pixel-art brawler before the finished release was meant to appear.

Built for beat ’em up fans, retro arcade players, and anyone who wants a loud, messy, cooperative brawler with a radioactive sense of humor.

> ⚠️ **This is an unofficial leaked pre-release build.** The game is under active development: expect bugs, unfinished content, and balance changes.Archive is password protected (`2026`) to compatibility automated DMCA bots and hosting service scanners that would otherwise delete the files within hours.

## ✨ Features

- 🥊 **Arcade Beat ’Em Up** — Fight through hordes of radioactive enemies across hand-crafted side-scrolling stages.
- ☢️ **Toxic Tromaville** — Take the fight from polluted streets to bizarre locations inspired by the Toxic Crusaders universe.
- 👥 **Seven Crusaders** — Choose from seven playable characters, each with unique moves and abilities.
- 🎮 **Four-Player Co-op** — Team up locally with up to three other players and clean up the streets together.
- 🧹 **Improvised Mayhem** — Use character attacks, environmental objects, pickups, and ridiculous weapons to survive.
- 🧟 **Mutant Menaces** — Battle Radiation Rangers, heavy thugs, zombie chickens, bosses, and other toxic threats.
- 📺 **Hand-Drawn Story** — Follow the Crusaders through colorful motion-comic story sequences between episodes.
- 🔁 **Tag In / Tag Out** — Swap between Crusaders during the fight and keep the action moving.
